03 / The fine print, up front

Evidence over confidence.

Why does this differ from other guides?

Game8 says a North American store table includes Japanese. Sony's US support table checked here does not. We keep that conflict visible and use the regional official table for checker results. We do not claim one universal language list covers every version.

Read the source comparison →
What did we verify for Xbox?

The Japan and US Xbox pages use the same product ID, 9P3H4968GRSM. Their embedded product data marks Japanese interface and subtitles supported, Japanese audio unsupported, and English audio supported. This is published listing evidence, not a test of a US-account purchase or every retailer edition.

Is this live, and what data does it collect?

No. It is a dated snapshot, checked on 19 November 2026. Results are calculated on your device; there is no backend, tracking, account or purchase flow. Nothing automatically refreshes the sources. External source links take you to the publishers' websites.

Sources and checking method

Official regional support tables drive PS5 results. The Japan digital Ultimate Edition listing provides additional corroboration. Secondary guides are context, not the data authority. "Not listed" means absent from that table, not a hands-on finding of impossibility.
