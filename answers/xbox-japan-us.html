GTA VI / Cross-region buying questions

Does the US Xbox version list Japanese subtitles?

2026-11-19 02:56 JST · Listing evidence, not a purchased-copy test.

Yes. Both the Japan and US Xbox product data list Japanese interface and subtitles.

The official Japan and US Xbox Store pages point to product 9P3H4968GRSM. Each page embeds a languagesSupported matrix for that product. In both, Japanese has interface and subtitles marked supported, with audio marked unsupported. English audio is supported.

Why this is stronger than a language count

The initial preview could only read the headline count of 13 languages. We have now checked the per-language data embedded in the official pages. The checker uses that matrix for Xbox Japan and US results.

What still needs checking before paying

The same product ID is not proof that every account, retailer code or regional entitlement behaves identically. We did not buy with a US account, redeem an imported code or test the game. Do not use the PS5 US language table to answer an Xbox question.
