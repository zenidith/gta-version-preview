GTA VI language answers

Can you change the subtitle language in-game?

Checked 19 November 2026 · Regional listing evidence, not hands-on testing

The exact GTA VI language-switching steps are not verified in this preview.

The official PlayStation support pages establish a language list, but the pages reviewed here do not explain a GTA VI-specific language-selection menu. A list of available languages is not proof that audio, menus and subtitles can be changed independently.

Do not use GTA V instructions as GTA VI evidence

This preview does not copy the older game's console-language instructions or invent a Settings menu path. It also does not claim a console-language change can add a language omitted from the relevant regional listing.

Before relying on a switching method, look for instructions for GTA VI itself from Rockstar or PlayStation, or check the actual game's settings. Hands-on verification is still needed here.
