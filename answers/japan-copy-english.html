GTA VI language answers

Does the Japan version have English?

Checked 19 November 2026 · Regional listing evidence, not hands-on testing

Yes, according to Sony's Japan listings.

The Japan PlayStation Support page lists English in both its Voice and Text columns. It also lists Japanese for text, without a voice mark.

The Japan PlayStation Store page for the Ultimate Edition separately lists English audio and Japanese/English screen languages. That is useful corroboration for the digital product shown there.

What this does not prove

This prototype has not tested a purchased copy. The support table calls its column "Text", not a separate subtitle-by-subtitle breakdown. We therefore show English text as listed, without promising the exact in-game menu path or coverage of every spoken line.

This answer applies to the Japan PS5 listings checked here, not automatically to Xbox or a copy sold in another region.
