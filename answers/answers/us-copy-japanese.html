GTA VI language answers

Does the US version list Japanese subtitles?

Checked 19 November 2026 · Regional listing evidence, not hands-on testing

Japanese is not in the US PlayStation Support language table.

As checked for this preview, the US support table lists 12 text languages and English voice. Japanese is absent. The Japan support table does list Japanese text.

Why some guides say otherwise

Game8's comparison guide says a North American store language table includes Japanese. That conflicts with the US PlayStation Support table we checked. Famitsu's November 18 reporting describes the Japan/overseas PS5 listing difference.

We do not turn the omission into a universal claim that every overseas copy cannot run Japanese. We report each regional official table separately. If Japanese is essential, the US table does not provide the confirmation you need: check the exact product and ask the seller before paying.

Xbox is different: its Japan and US product data both list Japanese interface and subtitles. Do not apply the PS5 US result to Xbox.
